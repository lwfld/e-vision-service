We plan, coordinate and staff factory and laboratory projects in Germany – from the first feasibility study to construction management on site.

## What we do

1. **Project management, concept design and feasibility studies** – layout, process flow and investment planning
2. **Construction management** – steering trades, schedules and costs
3. **Site supervision, HSE coordination and permit handling** – including communication with the authorities

## Your benefit

We know German permitting procedures and construction standards, and we communicate in the language of the customer as well as that of the authorities – shortening paths and avoiding costly misunderstandings.
