We support battery energy storage systems (BESS) and photovoltaic systems across their full life cycle – from the initial cost analysis through construction and installation to testing, maintenance and renovation.

## What we do

1. **Life-cycle cost analysis** – a sound basis for decisions before you invest
2. **Construction and installation of battery energy storage systems** – built by our own teams
3. **Maintenance and renovation** of photovoltaic and energy storage systems
4. **Warehouse handling, unpacking and testing of battery modules** – including incoming inspection and test runs

One partner for engineering and logistics: we combine technical expertise with our own warehouse infrastructure and dangerous-goods know-how – a decisive advantage for battery projects.
