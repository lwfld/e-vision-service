We source, import and install equipment – and provide the hands-on services that keep a production site running.

## What we do

1. **Import and export; goods and equipment procurement** – including customs handling
2. **Labour and on-site services** – cleaning, maintenance, assembly
3. **Equipment rental** – cleaning vehicles, forklifts, aerial work platforms

## Our teams on your site

Our own skilled workers, managed by us and working to German standards:

- **Forklift & transport operators** – certified drivers for internal logistics, loading and yard movements
- **Technical cleaning teams** – factory and machine cleaning with our own equipment and trained staff
- **Maintenance technicians** – experienced technicians for the upkeep of production equipment
- **Assembly workers & electricians** – skilled crews for line installation, relocation and commissioning
- **Production support staff** – auxiliary personnel for the production process, incl. catering and accommodation

## Your benefit

Equipment, labour and services from a single source – you coordinate one partner instead of five trades.
