Inbound logistics and testing support for a battery energy storage project – receiving, unpacking and testing modules before installation, with our own warehouse and site teams.

## Services provided

1. **Warehouse handling** – receipt, storage and staging of modules and components
2. **Testing equipment** – procurement and installation of module testing equipment
3. **Module testing and unpacking** – incoming inspection and test runs before hand-over
4. **Labour services** – operators and technicians provided and managed by E-Vision
