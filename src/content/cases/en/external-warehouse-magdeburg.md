Complete set-up of an external warehouse – from cleaning and fit-out to moving in and installing the production line, with our own teams and equipment.

## Services provided

1. **Factory cleaning** – cleaning equipment and professional cleaning personnel
2. **Fit-out design and procurement** – fencing, break room, office furniture and equipment
3. **Production line transport and installation** – forklifts, trucks, container unloading; operators and electricians
4. **Equipment rental** – factory cleaning vehicles, forklifts and aerial work platforms
